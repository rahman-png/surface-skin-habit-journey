# Spesifikasi video pengganti — Surface Skin Habit

Tujuan: gerakan halus seperti video saat ini, dengan tubuh lengkap dari kepala hingga pinggang. Kedua bahu, lengan atas, siku, pergelangan dan jari tetap berada di dalam frame selama seluruh animasi.

Prompt produksi:
Use the supplied woman and current video as identity and motion references. A continuous 10-second photorealistic skincare shot, locked camera, centered waist-up framing. Start with acne-prone skin and one hand covering half her face; slowly lower the hand to uncover her face; skin gradually becomes clear and glowing; end smiling happily with both hands supporting her chin. Keep the entire head, shoulders, both upper arms, elbows, wrists and all fingers fully inside the frame at every moment, with at least 15% horizontal safety margin on both sides. Frame extends down to the waist; only the body below the waist is outside the image. No camera zoom, pan or reframing. Same modest white top throughout. Pure white background, consistent soft studio lighting, natural skin texture, stable anatomy. Landscape 4:3 or 16:9 canvas, minimum 1440 pixels wide, 24 or 30 fps. No text, logo or watermark.

Catatan: video saat ini sudah memotong sisi lengan. Mengubah CSS/object-fit tidak dapat memulihkan anatomi di luar rekaman. Gunakan video dengan framing baru atau proses video outpainting yang menjaga konsistensi antar-frame.
